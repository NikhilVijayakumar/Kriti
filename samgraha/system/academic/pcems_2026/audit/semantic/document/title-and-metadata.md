# Semantic Rubric: Title and Metadata

## Scoring Criteria

### C1: Title Specificity
- **criterion_id**: C1
- **points**: 20
- **mandatory**: false
- **description**: Does the title specifically name the technique/system/contribution rather than describing the general problem area only?
- **pass_condition**: Title names a specific method, system, or contribution (not generic like "A Study on X")

### C2: Keyword Quality
- **criterion_id**: C2
- **points**: 15
- **mandatory**: false
- **description**: Do the keywords reflect the paper's content — identifying domain, method, and application?
- **pass_condition**: Keywords include domain-specific terms, method names, and application area

### C3: No Unsupported Superlatives
- **criterion_id**: C3
- **points**: 15
- **mandatory**: true
- **description**: Is the title free of unsupported superlatives ("novel," "first," "groundbreaking")?
- **pass_condition**: No superlatives in title, or superlatives are substantiated by contributions

### C4: Author/Affiliation Format
- **criterion_id**: C4
- **points**: 10
- **mandatory**: false
- **description**: Are author names in correct format with matching superscript institution numbers?
- **pass_condition**: Author names formatted correctly, superscripts match affiliations

### C5: Metadata Completeness
- **criterion_id**: C5
- **points**: 10
- **mandatory**: true
- **description**: Is the metadata block complete (title, authors, affiliations, email, keywords)?
- **pass_condition**: All five metadata elements present and non-empty
