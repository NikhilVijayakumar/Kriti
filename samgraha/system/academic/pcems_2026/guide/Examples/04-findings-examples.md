# Findings Examples

> *Source: PCEMS 2026 Sample Papers*

## Purpose

The findings section presents data, not claims. This document shows effective results presentation from accepted sample papers with annotations on what works and what could improve.

## Example 1: Comparative Results Table (Credit Card Fraud Detection)

### Table Presentation

> **Table 1. Performance of ML models with test size 20%**
>
> | Approach | Test size | Accuracy | Recall | F1-score | Precision |
> |----------|-----------|----------|--------|----------|-----------|
> | Naive bayes | 20% | 99.30 | 0.68 | 0.25 | 0.15 |
> | Logistic regression | 20% | 99.90 | 0.73 | 0.72 | 0.70 |
> | Random forest | 20% | 99.95 | 0.74 | 0.84 | 0.97 |
> | Decision trees(entropy) | 20% | 99.91 | 0.76 | 0.73 | 0.71 |
> | Decision trees(GI) | 20% | 99.92 | 0.77 | 0.76 | 0.76 |
> | SVM (linear) | 20% | 99.85 | 0.64 | 0.46 | 0.36 |

**What works**:
- Multiple metrics reported (accuracy, recall, F1, precision)
- All methods compared under same conditions
- Table clearly labeled with caption
- Test size specified

**What could improve**:
- Standard deviations not reported
- Statistical significance not tested
- Class imbalance not addressed in metric selection

### Text Reference

> "The experimental results are shown in Table 1 and depicted in Fig 1."

**What works**:
- Both table and figure referenced
- Reader can find the data in two formats

## Example 2: Parameter Study (Nanotube JLFET)

### Channel Length Scaling Results

> "The drain current comparison for the 5nm device has been done taking quantum effect into consideration. It has been shown that there is a slight decrease in the drain current when quantum effect, essentially Direct Source to Drain tunneling is considered as shown in Fig.4. It can also be seen that the I_ON current increases considerably in 5nm channel length devices with quantum effects due to increase in the charge carriers [17]."

**What works**:
- Specific phenomenon identified (quantum effects)
- Direction of change stated (slight decrease)
- Physical mechanism explained (charge carrier increase)
- Figure referenced

### Table of Device Parameters

> **TABLE I: KEY PARAMETERS USED IN DEVICE SIMULATION**
>
> Presented as a reference table linking physical parameters to simulation results.

**What works**:
- Parameters clearly listed with symbols and values
- Consistent notation with text
- Enables reader to reproduce simulation

## Example 3: Performance Metrics (WVD Paper)

### Quantitative Comparison

> **TABLE I: SSE values of TFR generated by different methods for x_1(t)**
>
> | Methods | Clean | 15dB | 10dB | 5dB |
> |---------|-------|------|------|-----|
> | WVD | 1.530 | 1.610 | 1.719 | 2.270 |
> | SPWVD | 3.212 | 3.018 | 3.224 | 3.059 |
> | BJD | 2.799 | 3.147 | 3.337 | 3.178 |
> | CWD | 3.223 | 3.008 | 3.132 | 3.014 |
> | Proposed Method | 1.038 | 1.142 | 1.092 | 1.158 |

**What works**:
- Multiple conditions tested (clean + 3 noise levels)
- Metric defined in text (SSE = Sum of Squared Estimate of Errors)
- Lower values clearly better (stated in text)
- Proposed method shown last for comparison
- Clear improvement demonstrated

### Text Analysis

> "By comparing the values of performance measures, it is clear that the proposed method outperforms other methods."

**What works**:
- Direct comparison statement
- Supported by the table data
- Reader can verify the claim

**What could improve**:
- Statistical significance not tested
- No discussion of why the improvement occurs

## Example 4: Prototype Results (IoT Warehouse)

### Observed Values Table

> **TABLE II: OBSERVED VALUES AND MEASURES TAKEN**
>
> | Parameters | Sensor Used | Wheat safe value | Wheat observed | Rice safe value | Rice observed |
> |------------|-------------|------------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
> | Temperature(C) | DHT11 | < 35 | 29 | < 32 | 35 |
> | Relative Humidity (%) | DHT11 | < 45 | 44 | < 70 | 44 |
> | CO2 levels (ppm) | MQ135 | < 500 | 380 | < 550 | 520 |

**What works**:
- Safe thresholds provided alongside observed values
- Multiple grains tested (wheat, rice)
- Sensor model identified
- Reader can immediately assess whether values are within safe range

### Graphical Presentation

> "Fig. 5 depicts the safe value and observed value for the wheat and rice."

**What works**:
- Bar chart comparison of safe vs. observed values
- Visual immediately communicates whether parameters are within range
- Both grains shown side by side

## Findings Section Checklist

- [ ] Results presented in tables with clear headers
- [ ] All metrics defined before first use
- [ ] Every table and figure referenced in text
- [ ] Comparison with baselines included
- [ ] Results analyzed, not just presented
- [ ] Physical or practical significance discussed
- [ ] Limitations of results acknowledged
- [ ] Figures legible in grayscale
- [ ] Axes labeled with units
