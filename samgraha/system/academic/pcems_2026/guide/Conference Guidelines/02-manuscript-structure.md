# Manuscript Structure

The manuscript should be organized as a single-column document.

## Title

- Arial
- 14 pt
- Bold
- Center aligned

---

## Authors

- Arial
- 12 pt
- Bold
- Center aligned

Include

- Author names
- Institution numbers

---

## Affiliations

- Arial
- 11 pt
- Center aligned

Include

- Institution names
- Corresponding author email

---

## Keywords

Provide at least four to five keywords.

Format

- Arial
- 12 pt
- Bold label

---

## Main Sections

The manuscript should contain the following major sections.

- Introduction
- Methodology
- Findings
- Conclusion
- References

Equivalent section names may be used where appropriate.

Subsections are allowed.
