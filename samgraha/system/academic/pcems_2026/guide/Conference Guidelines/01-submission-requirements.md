# Submission Requirements

## Originality

The submitted manuscript must:

- Be original research.
- Not have been published previously.
- Not be simultaneously submitted to another journal, conference, or workshop.
- Remain exclusive to PCEMS during the review period.

Violation of these requirements may result in immediate rejection without review.

---

## Submission

Authors must:

- Submit the manuscript electronically.
- Complete submission before the official deadline.
- Use the official conference template.
- Submit the manuscript as a Microsoft Word document.

---

## Paper Scope

Only full-length original research papers are accepted.
