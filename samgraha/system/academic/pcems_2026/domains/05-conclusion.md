# 05. Conclusion

**Domain:** `conclusion`
**Audit Target:** The generated conclusion section.

## Standard Definition

A concise close: restate the contribution, reinforce the empirical
validation, note practical impact, and identify future work. For PCEMS 2026,
the Conclusion follows a summary-forward pattern: contribution recap → key
findings → implications → future work. No new claims, no new citations, no
new data. The conclusion should be proportionate to the introduction — a
conclusion longer than the introduction is a structural smell.

### Expected Evidence (Deterministic)

1. **Word count within range:** 150–400 words (per `Writing Guide/
   06-conclusion.md`, target 200–300).
2. **No citations appear** — conclusions synthesize, they don't introduce
   new supporting evidence; a citation marker here is mechanically
   flaggable.
3. **No new numeric results** — no number appears here that isn't already
   in the Findings section.
4. **Future work present** — per sample paper analysis: 8 of 11 papers
   include future work directions.
5. **No placeholder text:** no `TODO`, `XXX`, or similar unfilled markers.

### Semantic Judgment Criteria

- Does the restated contribution match what the Introduction originally
  promised, or has scope drifted across the paper?
- Is the practical-impact statement specific to this work, or a generic
  closing sentence that could apply to any paper in the field?
- Are future work directions specific and actionable (e.g. "real-time
  detection systems") rather than vague ("more research is needed")?
- Does the conclusion connect back to the problem stated in the
  Introduction?
